# Geometry Dash

![[GeometryDash.png| 400]]

## Geometry Dash Overview

Geometry Dash is an indie game released in 2013. It is a side-scrolling rhythm platform video game developed by a single person named Robert Topala a Swedish game developer and his company RobTop Games. The game is playable for mobile devices and PC devices. This game challenges players to complete fast paced levels by jumping, flying, and avoiding obstacles with sync music. geometry Dash features amazing level designs, with simple one touch or one tap controls depending on what device your playing on. Geometry Dash also has a level editor that allows players to create custom levels to be shared in the Geometry Dash Community. 

![[G-D-4.png]]

## Geometry Dash Gameplay

Geometry Dash is a game where players control a geometric icon that always moves forward. Players have to click or tap at the right time to jump over spikes, hazards, walls, and navigate fast paced levels with challenging obstacles. Players are able to customize their geometric icon into many colors and shapes. Throughout some levels the player's icon can change into different forms, which changes the gameplay mechanics. That can include a cube, spiky ball, UFO, ship, spider, robot, a mouse cursor, with each its own unique movements.
