# Stray

![[Stray-Game-Cover.jpg]]

## Stray Overview

Stray is an indie adventure game released in 2022 by BlueTwelve Studio and published by Annapurna Interactive. The game is about a stray orange cat that becomes separated from its cat family and ends up into a unknown underground city inhabited by robots. The Stray cat must untangle an ancient mystery to escape a long-forgotten city with the help of a small flying drone named B-12, who happens to be a human who's conscious was transferred into the drone hundreds of years ago. 

![[Characters-from-Stray.png]]

## Stray Gameplay

Stray is a 3rd person cat adventure single player game allowing players to play as stray cat. Players get to control a stray cat through environments within the underground city. It is filled with puzzles, platforming, and stealth mechanics. Playing as a cat players are able to jump across rooftops, knock objects, scratch objects, climb pipes, and more while using realistic cat behaviors. With the B-12 companion alongside the cat, B-12 translates robot language, store away collected items, and helps environmental puzzles.

## Why I Like Stray

I like this game because it was the first time for me to play a single player adventure game as a cat, which was a unique experience for me to try. Playing as a cat felt like a new experience and it showed what is it like to play as a cat. Interacting with the environment in the game reflected real cat behavior. Overall, Stray became one of my favorite video games.
