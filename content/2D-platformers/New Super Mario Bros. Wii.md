# New Super Mario Bros. Wii
![[New-Super-mario-bros-wiiCover.jpg]]

## Overview of New Super Mario Bros. Wii

New Super Mario Bros. Wii is a side scrolling 2D platformer game for the Nintendo Wii in 2009. It is a single player experience and a multiplayer experience. It has a multiplayer mode that allows you play with 4 other players. The story of this game is about Mario, Luigi, Yellow Toad, and Blue Toad who need to rescue Princess Peach from Bowser and Bowser Jr. and the Koopalings. The game contains eight worlds and a secret hidden world.

![[mariobroswii-22.png]]

## Gameplay

In this game players get to play as four different characters for instance Mario, Luigi, Yellow and Blue Toad. Players get to run, jump, ground pound enemies, collect coins throughout each level. For each level players must reach the flag at the end of level before the time runs out. In the multiplayer mode it is the same concept, but you have from other players to work together to complete levels while competing with each other. Each Character gets to use powerups throughout the games such as Propeller Mushroom, Ice and Fire Flower, and the penguin suit, each providing different abilities. Players can also get to ride Yoshi, which helps make difficult obstacles easier to overcome.
