## Overview of Donkey Kong Country Returns HD

Donkey Kong Country Returns is a 2D platformer game created by Retro Studios and Nintendo. It was released for the Wii in 2010 and a 3DS version of the game later released in 2013, and later remade for the Nintendo Switch in 2025. The story of the game is about Donkey Kong and Diddy Kong as set out for a mission to recover their stolen bananas from a villain known as the Tiki Tak Tribe. Throughout the game you get to travel through different environments such as beaches, volcanoes, caves, jungles, while overcoming obstacles and defeating enemies. This game served as a revival of the classic Donkey Kong Country series game.

![[donkey-kongcountry.png]]
## Donkey Kong Country Returns Gameplay 

Donkey Kong is a side scrolling 2D platformer that involves fast paced movement, exploration, and more. It is similar to New Super Mario Bros Wii but its different in many ways. Players get to play as Donkey Kong and in some levels you have Diddy Kong joining the adventure, who provides additional abilities such as using his jetpack to reach longer distances. As Donkey Kong you are able to jump, run, roll, climb vines, ride mine carts and rocket barrels, and blast through barrel cannons, and ride a rhino. Your also collecting bananas, gold coins, puzzle pieces, and hidden K-O-N-G letters throughout each stage or level. Each world offers new mechanics and difficult challenges for users to overcome, while fighting unique villains throughout.

## Why I like this Game

I like playing this game a lot because of its amazing level design. Each level felt refreshing and brought new challenges, which made the game more fun for me. I enjoyed playing the rocket barrel levels because it required skill and accuracy to beat, if I died one time I start all over again and again. The soundtrack is another reason I like this game because it had enjoyable music to hear for each level. Overall, Donkey Kong Country Returns is one of my favorite games I have played.
