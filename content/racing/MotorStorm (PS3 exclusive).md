## Overview of the MotorStorm Franchise exclusive to the PlayStation Franchise

MotorStorm is an off-road racing game series that was developed by the now-defunct Evolution Studios and published by Sony exclusively for PlayStation platforms. The first game of the series released in Japan in 2006 and North America in 2007. It released four games in total, and it was known for its intense races and graphics, but unlike traditional racing games that focused on paved tracks, MotorStorm contained dangerous environments deserts, destroyed cities, snow terrains, jungles, and more. Players were able to choose from many different vehicle types, such as motorcycles, trucks, ATVS, Rally Cars.

## MotorStorm Gameplay

MotorStorm is different from other racing games because one of its unique features is that each vehicle type has its own strengths and weaknesses. Motorcycles are fast and easy to steer but are fragile, while large vehicles such as trucks are slower but are stronger, it also contains a boost and overheat mechanic. Holding the boost to long can overheat the engine, either slowing you down or causing a explosion. 

### MotorStorm Games in the Series

- **MotorStorm (2006)**: First game in the franchise and the original off-road festival set in Monument Valley.
- **MotorStorm: Pacific Rift (2008)**: Shifted the setting to a lush, tropical island.
- **MotorStorm: Arctic Edge (2009)**: Brought the festival to freezing Alaskan environments.
- **MotorStorm: Apocalypse (2011)**: Moved the racing into a collapsing, earthquake-ravaged city. 
- **MotorStorm: 3D Rift (2010)**: A shorter, 3D-enabled spin-off featuring tracks from _Pacific Rift_.
- **MotorStorm: RC (2012)**: A top-down, remote-controlled car spin-off for the PlayStation 3 and PlayStation Vita.

> MotorStorm is often remembered as one of PlayStation 3's most impressive racing franchises from a visual standpoint.

## The Legacy of MotorStorm

Despite the series not having released a new game in many years with the last release back in 2012, It was still considered to be one of the best arcade racing franchises out there for its destructive environments and gameplay.
