## Overview of Forza Horizon Series on Xbox Consoles

The Forza Horizon Series on Xbox is an open world racing game series developed by the Studio Playground Games and published by Xbox Game Studios. The Forza Motorsport series is also part of the Forza Horizon Racing series, it focuses on circuit racing such as F1 created by Turn10 Studios and published by Xbox Game Studios.

## Gameplay for both Forza Horizon and Motorsport series

In the Forza Horizon Series, players are able to drive on a open world environment, completing activities such as street racing, off-road racing, quests, missions, customize lots of vehicles, and more. In the Forza Motorsport series, it focuses on strictly on circuit racing, there are no open world elements, includes vehicle tuning, performance, closed tracks.
