## Overview of the Sonic Racing Series

The Sonic Racing Series is a series of racing games based on the Sonic the Hedgehog franchise created by Sega. The first game in the racing series was Sonic Drift that released in 1994 on the Sega Game Gear. However the series is more popular with the release of Sonic & Sega All-Stars Racing Transformed and other games such as Sonic Riders: Zero Gravity. You can play as many characters within the Sonic and Sega franchise such as Sonic, Shadow, Knuckles. 

![[SonicRacing-0.png]]
## Gameplay

Players race against each other using different characters and vehicles either in vehicles or hoverboards. During races, players can collect items, speed boosts, defensive abilities to help you against your racing opponents.
