## Overview of Need For Speed Franchise

The Need for Speed franchise is one of the most popular racing game franchises out there. Since the first game in 1994 to the latest game NFS Inbound in 2022, the series has been known for its fast paced street racing, intense police chase pursuits, and vehicle customization. As time passed by and each new game release each new installment introduced new gameplay mechanics locations, car collections while maintaining the high speed racing.
