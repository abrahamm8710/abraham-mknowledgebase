# Shadow The Hedgehog
![[Shadow-hedgehog2005cover.jpg]]


## Overview of Shadow The Hedgehog
Shadow The Hedgehog also called Shadow '05' is a spin off Sonic The Hedgehog game developed by Sega Studio USA in late 2005. The game contains traditional Sonic game elements while introducing third person shooter mechanics. Shadow is able to use guns to defeat enemies and get through levels and influence the level and plot selection. The story is focused on Shadow who doesn't remember who he is and has amnesia, all he remembers is a slim memory of Maria Robotnik who died in front of his eyes. The game allow players to help Shadow remember who he is. 
![[Shadow-Hedgehog2005.jpg]]

## Gameplay

Shadow The Hedgehog 2005 is a 3rd person shooter game, that follows the same concept as past 3D sonic games. Players get to control and play as Shadow the Hedgehog throughout the game, at some point in the game you can team up with other Sonic characters such as Sonic and Tails. Players are also able to collect rings, fight enemies, and run through stages at a fast pace. As Shadow players are able to drive his iconic motorcycle and military vehicles at certain parts of levels. 

![[Shadow05-gps.jpg]]

## Why I Enjoyed This Game

I enjoyed this game a lot because it was the first Sonic game to ever allow a character to use a gun, it was something new at the time it released, I thought I would see a gun in a Sonic game. The dark tone of the game and its awesome soundtrack set it apart from other Sonic games, which made the game exciting for me to play. Overall, Shadow the Hedgehog 2005 turned out to be one of my favorite video games ever.
