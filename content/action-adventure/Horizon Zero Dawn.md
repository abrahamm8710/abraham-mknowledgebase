# Horizon Zero Dawn
![[Horizon-Zero-DawnCover.jpg]]

## Horizon Zero Dawn Overview

Horizon Zero Dawn is a open world action adventure game developed by Guerilla Games and published by Sony/PlayStation for the PlayStation 4 in 2017. For Guerilla Games this was the first game of this new IP. The game is takes places in the 31st century in a post-apocalyptic Earth where machines dominate the Earth and wipe out the human race hundreds of years ago before the birth of Aloy a skilled huntress who was not wanted by her tribe because of her origin story. Players get to play as Aloy, who uses her skills to stay alive and protects herself and others against the machines. The game itself features sci-fi and mythical elements. 

![[Zero-DAwn-1.jpg]]

## Gameplay 
Horizon Zero Dawn is played from a third person perspective by combining open world elements such as combat, exploration, combat and role playing elements. Players control Aloy throughout different areas of the open world such as ruined cities of the 20th century, mountains, deserts, forests, and more. Throughout the game players also get to complete main  story missions, side questions, and activities. They are also able to upgrade weapons, suits, and health. When playing as Aloy, she has a device from the 20th century called a Focus that provides a augmented reality display when in use. It is used throughout the game to identify unknown machines, highlights enemy patrol routes, and points out points of interest. Aloy gets to use a variety of weapons such as bows, traps, tripcasters, ancient military weapons such as machine guns. As you progress through the game you upgrade your skills and skill tree by unlocking abilities in order to improve combat and exploration.

## Why I like Horizon Zero Dawn

I enjoyed this game because it offered a interesting story set in sci-fi post apocalyptic world, while also being set in a era of ancient tribes. Combining two different worlds made it much more interesting for me to play this. Battling machines was my favorite part of the game because I like the concept of destroying robotic machines. Overall, the game is one of my favorite games that I ever played.
