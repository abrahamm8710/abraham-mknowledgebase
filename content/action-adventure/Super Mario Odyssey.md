# Super Mario Odyssey 

![[SuperMarioOdysseyCover.jpg]]

## Overview of Super Mario Odyssey

Super Mario Odyssey released in 2017 is a 3D action adventure game released exclusively for the Nintendo Switch. The game features sandbox like elements similar to earlier games such as Super Mario 64 and Super Mario Sunshine. The game revolves around Mario exploring and traveling across kingdoms alongside his new companion Cappy to rescue Princess Peach from being forced to marry Bowser. 

![[SuperMarioOdysseyGP1.jpg]]

## Gameplay

In this game you get to play as Mario alongside Cappy from a 3rd person perspective. You also have to explore, platform, and collect Power Moons that hidden throughout each Kingdom. Unlike previous games this game doesn't have a central hub world, but instead uses a map screen where locations are selected. For Mario, players get to use his move set from previous 3D Mario Games, such as the Triple Jump, Long Jump, Roll, Ground Pound, Spin Jump, and more. With Cappy alongside Mario, Cappy can help players reach difficult obstacles and interact with certain objects and enemies so Mario can take control of them.  Even though this game is mostly a 3D game, certain sections of the game switch into a 2d side scrolling mode based on Super Mario Bros. 

![[SuperMarioOdysseyGP2.jpg]]

## Why I Liked Super Mario Odyssey

I like this game because it offered something new that wasn't seen in previous 3D Mario games. The addition of Cappy alongside Mario brought new gameplay mechanics that made the game fun and exciting to explore. It allowed you to experience each level in a different way. Overall, Super Mario Odyssey is one of my favorite video games because of its gameplay, level design, and exploration in the game.
