# Stellar Blade

![[Stellar-Blade-Coverart.jpg]]

## Overview of Stellar Blade

Stellar Blade is a single player science-fiction action adventure game developed by Shift Up and published by Sony. It was originally released for the PS5 in 2024. The game is about Eve, a female warrior sent from an off-world colony to reclaim Earth from unknown creatures such as Nayitbas. Throughout her mission she learns more about what happened with the human race. The game takes place in a post-apocalyptic environment, with powerful enemies and more. 

![[StellarBlade-GP1.jpg]]

## Stellar Blade Gameplay

Stellar Blade is played from the 3rd player perspective and it mainly focuses on fast-paced melee combat, exploration and character progression. Players get to play as Eve as she fights enemies with her sword, blocks attacks and strikes, and can carryout parries. Eve also has a companion drone that helps her throughout the game and it can transform into a range attack weapon. 
Eve is able to unlock and upgrade her skills throughout the game as well as equipment, and also has special abilities to deal significant damage to enemies. Players get to explore many post apocalyptic environments such as ruined cities, deserts, while completing story missions, side quests, collectibles, and fighting bosses. 

![[StellarBlade-Gp2.jpg]]

## Why I Like This Game

The reason I liked this game is because of its melee gameplay and detailed graphics. I enjoy the sword fighting in this game because it takes skill that when fight enemies with timing and precision, because a perfect hit would be rewarding while a mistimed attack would result in me getting hit. Stellar Blade's level design made it more enjoyable to play, it got you more curious about learning more about the world in Stellar Blade. Overall, this makes Stellar Blade one of my favorite games.
