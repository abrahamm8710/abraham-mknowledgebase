# Sonic X Shadow Generations
![[Sonic-X_ShadowGenCoverart.jpg]]

## Overview of Sonic X Shadow Generations

Sonic X Shadow Generations is an action adventure platforming game released in 2024 that was developed by Sonic Team and published by Sega. This game combines a remastered version of Sonic Generations that released back in 2011 with a all new campaign focused on Shadow the Hedgehog. Both players get to experience adventures featuring Sonic and Shadow as they run through iconic stages from the past and future while facing enemies and bosses. Sonic X Shadow Generations celebrates Sonic's history with improved visuals, gameplay, and additional content centered around Sonic's rival Shadow. 

## Gameplay

This game features two separate story campaigns that offer different gameplay experiences for Sonic and Shadow. For the Sonic campaign, players get to switch between Classic Sonic and Modern Sonic. You get to play side scrolling 2D platforms as Classic Sonic and fast paced 3D platforms as Modern Sonic through familiar stages, defeat enemies, collect rings, and fight bosses. This game offers quick reflexes, requires you to have precise movement and to move fast in, and a little of exploration for hidden rings and collectibles.

## Why I Enjoy This Game

I enjoyed this game because it allows to play as three different characters such as Modern Sonic, Classic Sonic, and Shadow who is one of my favorite characters. The fast paced gameplay makes it enjoyable for me because I like to finish the level at the highest time possible. The level design is makes every stage enjoyable for me to play, there is a lot of detail that went into this game. Overall, this game offers a lot of action and speed to make it one of my favorite Sonic games and video games in general.
