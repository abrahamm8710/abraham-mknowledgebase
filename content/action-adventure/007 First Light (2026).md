# 007 First Light
![[007FirstLightWallp.jpg]]

## Overview Of 007 First Light
 
Released in 2026, 007 First Light is based on Ian Fleming's secret British agent James Bond. The game is developed and published by IO Interactive, and is a action adventure game. 007 First Light tells a completely original origin story for James Bond rather than adapting stories from one of the films.  Players will take on the role of a young James Bond as he starts his career as a MI6 agent to complete dangerous missions around the world, while proving he has what it takes. The game combines cinematic story telling, action, espionage, and stealth. 

![[007FL.png]]

## Gameplay

007 First Light is played from a 3rd person perspective that combines stealth, combat, exploration, and spy craft, it is pretty much a 3rd person stealth action game. Playing as James Bond you infiltrate highly secured locations, solve puzzles, gather information, and complete missions using a variety of gadgets and weapons provided by MI6. Players can either engage in fast paced gun fights, hand to hand combat, sneak past enemies, and more. Throughout the game players get to control Bond as he travels to several international locations while completing missions. Players are able to use vehicles, technology, and use creative problem solving skills to complete missions. Players are able to choose how they want to play, that includes action, stealth, or both.

![[james_bond_gameplay_8f6388a440.png]]

## Why I Like This Game

The reason I like this game a lot is because it of its gameplay style. The idea of combining action, stealth, and exploration makes every mission feel different. Each mission I played was different it was either entering stealth mode, combat, or doing both. Some games really don't give you freedom to choose, but this since it allows you to choose, that is what makes this game special to me. No matter what option you chose the gameplay was exciting throughout the adventure. Overall, 007 First Light is one of my favorite video games that I have played.
