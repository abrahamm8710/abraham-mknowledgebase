# Sonic The Hedgehog 1991

![[276484-sonic-the-hedgehog-genesis-the-title-screen.png]]

## Overview Of The Game

Sonic The Hedgehog 1991 is a 2D retro classic platformer game for the Genesis and Mega Drive home video game systems. It is the first launch title debut of Sonic The Hedgehog, that was released in 1991 by Sonic Team and Sega. The story is about Sonic who races across South Island to stop Dr. Robotnik from capturing animals and transforming them into robots. Known for its classic graphics, gameplay, and soundtrack this game became a influential game during its time and helped create the Sonic Franchise

## Gameplay 

Sonic 1991 is a side scrolling platformer game that focuses on speed and skill to pass through levels. Players play as Sonic and run through a variety of zones filled with moving platforms, enemies, hidden paths, loops, springs, and more. When playing as Sonic you are able to jump and perform his iconic spin attack to defeat enemies and destroy obstacles, you also need to collects yellow rings to protect sonic from taking damage. You must avoid dangerous objects such as spikes, enemy attacks, bottomless pits while collecting the 7 Chaos Emeralds.
