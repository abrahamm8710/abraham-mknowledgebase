## Super Mario Bros (1985) Overview 

Super Mario Bros is a retro classic side scrolling platformer released in 1985 for the Nintendo Entertainment System (NES) and for the Family Computer. Despite not being the first Mario game of the Mario franchise, it introduced a lot of gameplay mechanics such as powerups, classic enemies, and the mushroom kingdom that still today in modern Mario games. The game is about Mario who goes on a mission to rescue Princess Peach (Princess Toadstool) from Bowser. Mario must past difficult obstacles, defeat enemies, and levels to rescue the princess. 

![[super-mario-bros-1985-banner.png]]

## Super Mario Bros Gameplay

This game is played from a 2D side scrolling perspective, where players get to play as Mario and navigate a variety of levels filled with obstacles, enemies, moving platforms, pits, and more. Mario is able to defeat enemies by jumping on them, and collect powerups such as the fire flower, and the iconic mushroom. The game contains 8 worlds with each world containing 4 levels. Players also get to play as Luigi, but still has the same play style as to Mario.
